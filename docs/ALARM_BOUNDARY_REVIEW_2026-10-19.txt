Finding and reuse
MAJOR: the host's service.Alarm receiver was exported without a permission or action
filter. An external explicit broadcast could reach a receiver that requests node
foreground-service startup and a health check. OS foreground-start restrictions may
reject some requests, but the app did not need or intend an external entry point.

Read app/src/main/java/com/eurobuddha/minimacore/service/Alarm.java in full, its
MinimaService scheduling calls and ResyncLauncher.afterShutdown caller. Hourly and
one-shot requests already use explicit immutable PendingIntents with separate codes
0 and1. Reused the existing private-receiver policy in integrated/NFTwallet's
HeartbeatReceiver/manifest, also present in standalone NFT Wallet, PandaPools,
PandaDEX and AtomiX. The production change is only exported=false plus its comment.
Alarm timing, cancellation, health checks, restart handling and service code are
unchanged. The host manifest is shared by all three bear flavors. This checkpoint
builds Pandamonium; other flavor APKs are not claimed newly verified.

Warnings triaged without speculative changes
The three UnsafeProtectedBroadcastReceiver warnings name NFT Wallet, PandaPools
and PandaDEX BootReceiver. Read their complete implementations and both integrated
and standalone manifests: all are already exported=false. Other apps cannot invoke
them directly, so the warning alone does not establish an externally callable bug.
AtomiX has the same private receiver policy. Main BootReceiver checks the protected
BOOT_COMPLETED action before starting an initialized node. Future Cash Next checks
its boot/update/quickboot action allowlist and configured rescue readiness. Its OEM
quickboot policy and real device reboot behavior remain wider review/device work.
No standalone boot receiver change is required by the confirmed host Alarm finding.
