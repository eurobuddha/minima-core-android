AtomiX saved-order identity repair — 2026-10-19

Review finding (MAJOR, fixed)
MainActivity.editOrderDialog loads Order.fromConfigJson, which deliberately stores
pairs only. pushOrderEdit passed that object to the publisher without the Minima
HTLC public key or Ethereum address. In Pandamonium, the funding/context guard
therefore rejected live edits. The standalone publisher could sign a payload with
those fields absent. The same omission affected the editor's withdrawal payload.

Reuse and fix
Reused the three assignments from MainActivity.baseOrder in the standalone APK
and integrated source: current Minima public key, current wallet address, cached
USDT display balance. Applied them after the existing readiness/identity checks
and before peg/funding work. Existing Pandamonium publishFresh still replaces the
cached balance with checked funding; no funding or authorization guard was removed.
Saved pairs and user-entered prices/amounts are retained. Other order publishers
(currency switch, background refresh, peg withdrawal, zombie-order withdrawal)
already attach their identities and were inspected for the same gap.
The host adapter scripts/adapt-pandamonium-atomix-bid-backing.py reproduces the
repair from pinned donor sources. No standalone AAR or generated binary is copied.
