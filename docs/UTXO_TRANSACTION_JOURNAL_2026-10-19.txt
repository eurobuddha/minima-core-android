Behavior
Database v8 adds durable attempts without dropping either history table. Exact
validated input/output economics are frozen and persisted before any node command.
POSTING is committed before signing/posting; exact receipt evidence is required
before POSTED. Uncertain submission retains input reservations and cannot be
replayed merely because a response was lost. Recovery requires matching transaction
contents and fresh three-block inclusion. Legacy matching excludes journalled
attempts. Unresolved audit rows cannot be deleted. Confirmed local rows remain
visible until their corresponding node-history record is cached.

Distribution persists its exact batch before submission and advances only after
that journal attempt is confirmed. Change selection requires exact output coin ID,
amount, address and token. Final batches without change also wait for confirmation.
Old jobs lacking frozen intent remain held. A distinct onNotStarted callback
records admission failure before any node command, so a rejected initial
reservation does not leave a new distribution permanently uncertain. Failed
persistence retains the hold. This additional admission fix also needs porting
back to NFT Wallet during the remaining parity work.

Native Minima consolidation uses the existing unsigned dry run, validates wallet
ownership and full economics, then submits through the same durable builder.
Coin loading validates every result before replacing displayed balances. Missing
human token amounts cannot fall back to raw units. Spent inputs and pending node
responses cannot establish transaction evidence.
