Problem and resulting behavior
UTXO NodeApi defaulted a missing/non-Boolean status to success. A malformed
transport result could advance construction or claim a submitted transaction.
A timeout scheduled txndelete while signing/posting might still be executing.
Native consolidation treated a missing reply as a definite failed transaction.

Both implementations now require a Boolean success envelope. Missing/invalid
results, reply overflow, timeout and transport exceptions after possible dispatch
reach onUnknown. Definite node rejection and exact known local not-sent errors
remain failures. Custom signing handles uncertainty explicitly without cleanup or
retry. Native consolidation stores UNKNOWN and refreshes History. Read callbacks
retain their existing error/retry handling through the default onUnknown callback.
Command setup is serialized on the main handler, a closed client cannot dispatch,
and late results after closure or timeout cannot complete a write twice.

Release-blocking work still open
- A Boolean-success reply is not itself a complete transaction receipt. UTXO's
  existing parseTxpowid local-handle fallback and partial output parser still need
  NFT TransactionEvidence/journal reuse.
- Distribution can still guess change from address/amount after an uncertain post,
  and its pre-post persistence/restart/final-batch handling needs the NFT journal
  integration. This checkpoint does not claim to repair distribution.
- Consolidation needs the prepared-intent/reservation flow; a lost native reply
  may leave insufficient economic evidence for automatic history recovery.
- Full immutable review/input reservation and standalone parity remain in progress.
