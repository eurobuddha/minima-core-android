Problem and repair
MAJOR: stored confirmed journal/display rows were presented as currently confirmed
without a fresh chain lookup. Node history also displayed a cached block number
without distinguishing saved evidence from current inclusion. A restart, offline
node or reorganization could leave that presentation misleading indefinitely.
Rows now say Saved, retaining their recorded block, amount, direction and detail.
Saved confirmed receipts say Previously confirmed and invite a recheck. Their
visual state no longer shows a green confirmation check without a fresh lookup.
Opening either cached node history or a local receipt starts a read-only
`txpow onchain:<id>` query. Each opening performs a new check; positive results
include the node-reported inclusion block/depth and time of the check. No inferred
confirmation count is derived from the saved TxPoW header or elapsed time.
Typed absence is shown as Not found on this node plus Outcome unverified, never
as a failed transfer. Malformed/failed replies show Outcome unverified; raw errors
are not rendered. No current status is persisted or used to change journal state.

Reuse and compatibility
Inspected complete wallet HistoryView/HistoryDb/NodeTx, LocalHistory and its tests,
TransactionRecovery, TransactionEvidence and callers. Compared standalone UTXO,
NFT, History and minimaWallet paths; searched shared minimaapi and integrated
helpers. Read core txpow.java and its retained-chain inclusion response.
Reused the asynchronous detail lookup/display from integrated/blockexplorer/
src/main/java/com/eurobuddha/blockexplorer/MainActivity.java, and the strict parser
in integrated/NFTwallet/.../TransactionEvidence.java. PandaPools ActivityLog/
CollectConfirmation and tests supplied the historical/check-time distinction.
The Explorer parser alone accepts incomplete evidence; the Pools helper is coupled
to its own persistent activity DB and payout queue. Neither can be directly used
as a wallet UI helper. HistoryChainCheck is the small adapter connecting the
existing wallet NodeApi, evidence parser and Language/TextView display.
The full inclusion parser is reused as observedInclusion. Existing inclusion still
requires depth>=3 for settlement. Display alone may report a fully validated
inclusion with0–2 confirmations. Typed status/found, nonzero block identity,
positive block and exact tip-minus-block depth checks are retained.

Safety and behavior
Invalid/local handles never become commands. Each callback is single-use and
rejects a closing/destroyed host. Existing NodeApi supplies timeout and main-thread
lifecycle delivery. Each dialog owns its label, so later dialog checks cannot be
replaced by a previous dialog's response. No DB access, receipt deletion,
reservation release, transaction replay, key use or signing occurs in the helper.
No history rows or local records are removed. Confirmed journal entries retain
historical settlement evidence; the display does not downgrade or reopen them.
