Finding and repair
MAJOR: MainActivity built usdtAvail from displayed balances; SwapService loaded it
from preferences. The final publisher only refreshed Minima-side funds. An exhausted
USDT balance could therefore refresh a bid using old funds. Readers returned a bid
ladder even when the signed payload's advertised USDT balance was zero.
All four live publication paths (manual, automatic foreground/background, edit)
now go through SwapEngine.publishFresh. Its existing executor reads balanceOf for
the captured wallet owner, checks the Ethereum chain and exact ABI response, and
uses the lower of latest and pending balances. Pending incoming funds alone cannot
back an order. Malformed/unavailable reads fail with the existing localized error;
they never reuse a cached amount. Ask-only orders and tombstones retain their
existing paths without requiring an Ethereum RPC balance read.
A bid consumes price * amount USDT. Exact BigDecimal accounting keeps only the
best-price prefix fully covered by available funds. Empty ladders also clear the
legacy scalar so it cannot resurrect a synthetic bid. Legacy scalars must cover
the configured minimum. Received orders apply the same clamp against advertised
funds. Saved configuration is untouched and can resume after funding returns.
Edit success messages reflect the resulting order, including an empty withdrawal;
feed-recovery notifications no longer claim liquidity for a depleted order.

Remaining safety review
An order is a snapshot, not escrow. RPC pending state may omit a locally saved
signed transaction whose broadcast outcome is unknown, or a transaction submitted
through another endpoint. The shared EthereumTransactions journal preserves those
attempts but this validator does not yet reserve their token amounts. Review and
connect durable pending-spend accounting before claiming full buy-side reservation
safety. Gas affordability and pending allowances also require separate coverage.
Long provider failover can delay publication; no stale-balance fallback is allowed.
Existing quotes require native postage to withdraw and can otherwise remain until
their normal expiry. Remote makers' signed balances remain untrusted assertions.
